Non-commercial pet project created for fun.

<p>This is a side project created to simulate the technology and design of various forums from around 2009, driven by nostalgia. All users and their dialogues are fictional, but the ideas and diversity are inspired by real people, their behavior, and different thoughts. It brings together a variety of characters who may disagree with each other's opinions, argue, or form friendships. In a way, this project could even be described as something close to a visual novel. Most of the character dialogues were written by hand, while their visual avatars were created using AI generation based on various prompts.</em> </p>

<p>This website is a simulation of real people, with just one difference:
It features a fake alien civilization that feels fear and anxiety over the thought that another alien species might try to contact them sooner or later. They have no desire to share their technology or receive any from outer worlds. However, among the humens, there are those who oppose this mindset.</em> </p>

<p>The terminology is close to real-world language, but certain words may look unfamiliar and alien.</em> </p>
